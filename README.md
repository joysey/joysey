## 🧠 What I'm Building

I build tools around a simple idea:

> **Your AI conversations should belong to you — not stay locked inside one platform.**

A growing amount of our research, writing, code, ideas, and decisions now lives inside conversations with AI.

My work focuses on helping people:

- capture AI conversations,
- export them into portable formats,
- keep long-term backups,
- organize them into projects and knowledge,
- and eventually rediscover and reuse what they have already learned.

## ⭐ Featured Project

### 🧠 AI Exporter Hub for Obsidian

Turn exported AI conversations into a **private, searchable, local-first knowledge hub** inside Obsidian.

**What it does**

- Browse ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and Genspark conversations in one place
- Organize conversations by project, category, tags, favorites, and review status
- Review new conversations through an Inbox
- Search your AI history
- Read conversations in a dedicated reader
- Rediscover older conversations
- Create Markdown knowledge notes from important ideas
- Keep your data entirely inside your Obsidian vault

> **Markdown is the source of truth.**

No account.  
No proprietary database.  
No telemetry.  
No automatic upload.

**Repository:** `joysey/ai-exporter-hub-obsidian`

## 🧠 How I Think About AI Knowledge

I currently think about the workflow in four layers:

```text
Capture
   ↓
Organize
   ↓
Retrieve
   ↓
Rediscover
```

**Capture**
Get conversations out of separate AI platforms.

**Organize**
Connect them to projects, categories, and context.

**Retrieve**
Find something when you remember that it exists.

**Rediscover**
Bring back something useful that you forgot existed.

That last layer is where I think personal AI knowledge systems become especially interesting.
